ABOUT US

Young4ChickS is an urban brooder initiative targeting youth aged 18–30. Founded by a Youth Member of Parliament, it aims to empower young urban dwellers by enabling them to participate in wealth creation through poultry farming.

MISSION

To stimulate wealth creation and economic involvement among youth through accessible poultry farming opportunities.

VISION

To become a leading youth-centered agricultural empowerment platform in urban settings.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Over 1,200 youth farmers supported since launch with digitized systems and transparent distribution.
